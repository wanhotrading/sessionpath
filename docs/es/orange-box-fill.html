ORANGE EXPANSION BOXES · EARLIER-ONLY FORECASTS · ES

Can both orange boxes fill?

Measure whether price reaches both projected expansion targets within a single SessionPath window: 03:00–05:00, 05:00–08:30 or 08:30–16:00.

Touch: the high reaches the upper orange outer edge and the low reaches the lower orange outer edge. Exceed: price strictly passes both outer edges. An outside close is not required. Strict exceedances also count as touches.

Making a new high and a new low alone is a different event. For a known high of 100, low of 99 and orange distances +0.50/−0.30, the full targets are 100.50 and 98.70.

Research conditions

All forecasts are fitted to strictly earlier dates. The headline requires at least 50 earlier matches. This removes most early-window dates before 2024 and some rare states afterward; see each row’s actual scored date range. Lowering this includes more sparse forecasts; their box widths can be less stable. No dates or NY methods are pooled as independent observations.

How the orange boxes were rebuilt

  1. Freeze the current extremes. At 03:00,05:00 or 08:30, use the highest high and lowest low since the previous calendar 19:30, including the known decision-time open. Later candle prices do not enter those anchors.
  2. Use the established research matching rules. For 03:00 and 05:00, use up to 50 nearest earlier matches with the same prior-NY BBOX quarter and previous-hour behaviour, ranked by five known numeric inputs. NY uses the selected Own/Prior Exact/Wide Asia/London closing-position cohort, using all eligible earlier matches.
  3. Measure each match’s future expansion. Upper expansion=max(future high−that match’s cutoff high,0)÷that match’s frozen AVR. Lower expansion=max(that match’s cutoff low−future low,0)÷AVR. Average each side separately, including zero, then multiply by the forecast day’s AVR.
  4. Draw the full orange targets. Upper target=current high+mean upper expansion. Lower target=current low−mean lower expansion. Preserve the six-decimal distances exported by the indicator code; do not round to a futures tick.
  5. Score one window only. Use completed candles [03:00,05:00), [05:00,08:30) or [08:30,16:00), New York time. The endpoint-opening candle is excluded. Both targets can occur in any order, including the same five-minute candle.
  6. Keep the denominators honest. Missing candles, unavailable earlier fits and pairs with a zero-height orange box are not failed trades or zero-move observations. Incomplete and sparse cases are reported separately.

This is a replay of the established historical research protocol. A live forecast made with different tags, inputs, checkpoint conditions or NY filters can have different orange targets. These are exploratory historical event rates, not trade win rates or a validated strategy. Wilson 95% intervals do not adjust for serial dependence or multiple comparisons.
