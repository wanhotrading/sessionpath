One ruler: the full NY forecast width

Let the ordinary Mean SessionPath lower extreme be L, its upper extreme H, and width W = H − L. The upward target is L + multiple × W; the downward target is H − multiple × W.

Research definitions and source data

The NY box is the ordinary Mean forecast for 08:30–16:00. Own and Prior methods classify the completed Asia/London inputs against their corresponding measuring range; Wide and Exact retain their established matching rules. Each method is reported separately.

Morning orange-tail conditions require both outer orange targets to be touched in the completed early window. 04:30 double breaks require strict breaches of both frozen 04:30 range edges before 08:30. The second side supplies the morning direction; unknown same-candle order is excluded.

This calculation uses strictly earlier forecast matches. Earlier Q7 tables used a different retrospective fitting setup, so their percentages should not be substituted for this study.
