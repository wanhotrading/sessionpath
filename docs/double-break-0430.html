Definitions and coverage

  1. Freeze the box: H and L from completed five-minute candles between the previous calendar 19:30 and 04:30. W=H−L. All clocks use New York time and daylight saving.
  2. Qualify: a high strictly above H and a low strictly below L during 04:30–08:30. Boundary touches alone do not count. These are wick breaks, even if the candle closes inside.
  3. Record the second break: the first candle that breaches the remaining side. The archive gives a five-minute interval, not an exact intrabar time. An 08:25 candle is confirmed at 08:30.
  4. Measure afterward: exclude the second-break candle, then use subsequent candles through 09:25 plus the 09:30 opening price. Never use the 09:30 candle’s later high or low.
  5. Project: U level=L+mW; D level=H−mW. For H=91 and L=89, 120% is 91.40 upward or 88.60 downward. These are range multiples, not standard deviations.
  6. New targets: exclude each date if that target was touched at any point before or during the confirming candle. Count a hit only afterward. “Added beyond known extreme” measures the new extension beyond the maximum already known at confirmation, including zero.
  7. Separate denominators: incidence uses all complete box and qualification windows. Forecast outcomes additionally need complete data through 09:30. Same-candle dual breaks would count in incidence but cannot identify a second direction and are excluded from directional results.

Intervals are unadjusted 95% Wilson intervals. Samples are small, especially after time and direction filtering. Serial dependence and many exploratory comparisons are not accounted for by those intervals. Data collection covers 2022–2026; this page does not claim a proven trading edge.

Reproducibility: each daily record retains contract, both breakout candle times, confirmation, fixed box size, post-signal extrema, known extrema, target status and first-hit times. Normalized source hashes are included in the JSON download. CL coverage audit · NQ coverage audit.
