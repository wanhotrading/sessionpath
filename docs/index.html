Session timing and shape definitions

All times are New York local time, including daylight saving time. *NTA uses available bars from the prior NY 16:00 close to the 19:30 Asia anchor. Normally 16:00–16:55 and 18:00–19:25; Monday includes Friday’s last hour and Sunday’s reopening. The closed period is not filled with invented bars. Asia is 19:30–02:00, London 03:00–08:30, NY 09:30–16:00 (end times excluded). The 02:00–03:00 and 08:30–09:30 handoffs are outside these shape labels.

Shapes retain the workbook rules. “Up/down finish” means a close in the upper/lower quarter with the corresponding order of extremes, not necessarily a positive/negative open-to-close return. Narrow range takes precedence. Hover a bar for its exact definition.

What happened to the data-quality tags?

They were evidence warnings, not market signals. Missing-bar cases are excluded from the affected calculation automatically. ORDER_CHECK flags uncertain ordering within a five-minute bar; COVERAGE_CHECK flags incomplete source coverage. Their notes remain attached to historical evidence instead of appearing as trading filters.
