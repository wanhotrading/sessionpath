SessionPath NY breakout timing and end-of-window study
Protocol fixed 2026-11-02 before new outcome summaries or predictive scores were computed.

Question: Does the first NY breakout's confirmation time, and/or the breakout candle's closing overshoot, improve forecasts of an untouched 1.50x same-side target and the 16:00 closing location beyond a side/type historical baseline? Separately describe how far later candles travel after breakouts at different times.

Sources: unchanged audited explicit-contract CL/NQ/ES/GC five-minute OHLC and saved earlier-only ordinary Mean NY forecasts. CL/NQ research endpoint 2026-10-15; ES/GC 2026-10-20. Four Own/Prior Exact/Wide methods remain separate. Primary method Own Wide with at least50 earlier forecast matches. Other three methods and all positive-fit forecasts are sensitivity/descriptive views, never additional independent dates. Saved forecast cohort dates must all be strictly earlier than forecast date. Serialize open and U/D distances to six decimals exactly as existing indicators; no tick rounding or box-width changes.

Window: [08:30,16:00) America/New_York. Exactly90 complete five-minute candles. End-of-window close is the15:55 candle's close at16:00; exclude the candle opening16:00. No missing-bar interpolation. Preserve no-break, ambiguous, sparse and rejected records.

Event definitions: WICK_FIRST is first strict high>upper or low<lower. If both first breaches occur in one candle, mark AMBIGUOUS and exclude from side-dependent tests. A WICK_FIRST event is WICK_ONLY if its completed close is inside inclusive boundaries; otherwise CLOSE_OUTSIDE. CLOSE_FIRST is an independent alternative event definition: first completed candle close outside either boundary. Do not mix the two definitions as independent observations. Each method/date/event definition contributes at most one event. Main predictive tests use WICK_FIRST only. CLOSE_FIRST is descriptive in this release.

Observation time: signal candle completion. Store its opening time separately. Half-hour category is floor(completion NY minute/30)*30. Earliest possible confirmation08:35 lies in08:30-08:59. Confirmation16:00 is terminal, distinct from15:30-15:59. Terminal signals have no future target or EOD forecast opportunity; retain their final known outcomes descriptively only.

Strength: closing overshoot = distance of completed signal close beyond the breached boundary / full Mean U+D width. Use requested10-percentage-point floor bands, e.g14%=10-<20%,27%=20-<30%. WICK_ONLY uses CLOSED_INSIDE, never a zero closing-overshoot category. Retain continuous closing overshoot and wick overshoot separately. Use only arithmetic tolerance near exact bin boundaries.

Range multiples: upward target L+m(H-L); downward H-m(H-L). Thus1.50x means50% of full width beyond the edge, not150% beyond it. Descriptive targets1.20,1.30,1.40,1.50,1.75,2.00. A target touched on any candle before/through confirmation is already reached. A next-opening price at/beyond target is a separate already-at-entry group. Pending requires a resolved side, an untouched target through confirmation AND next open, and at least one later candle. Numerator is later wick touch through16:00. Do not call the trigger-candle touch a future hit. For CLOSE_FIRST, prior wick hits count as already reached even if trigger candle itself does not hit the target.

EOD outcomes: final16:00 close INSIDE inclusive ordinary Mean box, SAME_SIDE outside relative to breakout side, or OPPOSITE_SIDE outside. Store signed closing position, close change from confirmation, and continuous boundary overshoot independently of target touch. A target hit can coexist with an inside EOD close. No result is a trade win rate.

Descriptive extension: subsequent maximum same-side overshoot beyond boundary, floored at zero, divided by full width. Report mean, median,p75,p90 and zero further increase beyond the trigger extreme. Also preserve actual later maximum multiple (may be inside), maximum full-session multiple, additional range beyond trigger extreme including zero, signal-candle target-hit rates, pending rates and EOD state counts. Report counts and95% Wilson intervals; raw intervals are descriptive and unadjusted for dependence/multiple comparisons.

Predictive comparisons: separate markets and outcomes. Main target is pending1.50x binary hit, Brier=(p-y)^2. EOD is three-state probability vector, Brier=sum over classes of(p_k-y_k)^2 on its0-2 scale; don't compare its magnitude with binary Brier. Baseline separately conditions on U/D and WICK_ONLY/CLOSE_OUTSIDE, with Laplace smoothing (binary Beta1,1; multiclass Dirichlet1,1,1). Fixed20-observation shrinkage; no parameter search or post-result regrouping. Candidate uses only information known at confirmation/next open. Do not cross AVR,BBOX,tail events or previous endpoint into these new tests.
