Data and interpretation

The existing archive and its frozen AVR define the sample. We verified the 08:30 and 16:00 values against the study inputs before measuring the new deadlines. The missing 14 CL timeline dates were verified against the original five-minute CLX2026 export. No candles were interpolated.

Rates summarize historical frequencies, and very small samples can produce wide intervals. The recent period is a descriptive comparison, not an independent test or a probability calibration. Hitting the range target does not tell you which side expanded or whether a trade would win.
