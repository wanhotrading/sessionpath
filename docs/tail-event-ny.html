Interpretation and audit

A higher extreme-touch probability does not establish a profitable earlier entry. Compare future favourable and adverse movement, then test a specified entry, stop and exit on later unseen dates. Waiting also leaves a shorter horizon, so falling touch rates alone do not show that waiting is worse.

No news cause has been assigned. The current sample describes price patterns, and overlapping events are not independent confirmations. Only pre-08:30 orange events enter this morning study; selecting days using the NY orange outcome would use future information.
